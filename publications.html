Publications

Research in print

My publications examine political communication, media and conflict, artificial intelligence and digital platforms, and higher education under war. They include peer-reviewed articles, book chapters, a policy plan and reports, in English and Arabic.

Recent work extends discourse analysis from war reporting to social media, multimodal communication and AI-mediated media, and to scholasticide and the reconstruction of Palestinian higher education.
