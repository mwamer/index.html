Academic leadership

Leading and serving academic institutions

Alongside research and teaching, I have led and served academic institutions and communities. I was Dean of the Faculty of Mass Communication and Languages at Gaza University from 2020 to 2023, I founded and first chaired the Middle East Chapter of the Marie Curie Alumni Association, and I contribute to scholarship through peer review and conferences.

This experience shapes my research on higher education in conflict-affected settings and my advice to universities on programmes, quality and institutional development.

My approach combines strategic planning with attention to the everyday conditions in which staff and students work. Institutional development depends on formal structures and policies, but also on collegial cooperation, academic capacity and an environment that enables research, teaching and intellectual exchange. It is also why I research the relationship between academic governance, institutional capacity and the long-term development of universities, a question central to rebuilding higher education in Gaza.

Networks

Academic networks

Founding and serving academic communities that strengthen exchange between researchers in the Middle East and international research communities.

2018–2020

Middle East Chapter, Marie Curie Alumni Association

I am the founder and first Chair of the Middle East Chapter of the Marie Curie Alumni Association (MCAA), which I chaired from 2018 to 2020. I trained candidates from the Middle East to prepare applications for Marie Skłodowska-Curie fellowships and organised workshops explaining the fellowships, strengthening academic exchange between researchers in the region and international research communities.
