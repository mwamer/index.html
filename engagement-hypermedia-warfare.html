Hypermedia Warfare and Techno-Legal Entanglements Symposium

July 2026 · Hypermedia Warfare and Techno-Legal Entanglements Symposium, Cambridge Digital Humanities, King's College, Cambridge, United Kingdom · Co-Organiser

Dr. Wesam Amer played a central co-organising role in the Hypermedia Warfare and Techno-Legal Entanglements Symposium at King's College, University of Cambridge, helping to convene leading global experts and shape an interdisciplinary programme examining the intersections of media, artificial intelligence, law, and contemporary warfare in an increasingly digital world.

The Hypermedia Warfare and Techno-Legal Entanglements Symposium, held at King's College, University of Cambridge, convened an international gathering of scholars, journalists, legal experts, technologists, and practitioners to examine the rapidly evolving relationship between media, artificial intelligence, law, and contemporary conflict.

A key figure behind the event was Dr. Wesam Amer, who co-organised the symposium alongside Henning Grosse Ruse-Khan and Ryan Heuser. Dr. Amer played an instrumental role in bringing together a diverse network of academics and practitioners, helping to shape an interdisciplinary forum dedicated to some of the most pressing issues facing conflict-affected societies today.

The symposium featured an outstanding lineup of keynote and plenary speakers, including Gillian Tett, Balakrishnan Rajagopal, Mohanad (Mo) Hashim, Neve Gordon, Janine di Giovanni, and Raji Abdul Salam. Under the framework developed by the organising team, participants engaged in discussions addressing artificial intelligence in armed conflict, hypermedia warfare, digital manipulation, algorithmic power, war crimes documentation, accountability, and the implications of international law in digitally mediated conflicts.

Dr. Amer's contribution was particularly significant in fostering dialogue across disciplinary boundaries. By helping to curate an environment that brought together expertise from media and communication studies, journalism, political science, law, technology, and human rights, he contributed to creating a platform capable of addressing complex global challenges from multiple perspectives.

Discussions throughout the day examined the realities facing societies affected by conflict, including Gaza, Ukraine, Sudan, and beyond, while exploring how emerging technologies continue to reshape information environments and patterns of warfare.

The symposium concluded with a strong emphasis on future collaboration and research. Through the leadership and organisational efforts of Dr. Amer and his fellow co-organisers, the event established a valuable space for knowledge exchange and set the foundation for continued interdisciplinary engagement on the interconnected dynamics of media, technology, law, and war.
