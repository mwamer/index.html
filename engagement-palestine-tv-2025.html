Media & Commentary

Palestine TV interview

10 April 2025 · Palestine TV, Madrid, Spain · Interviewee

Following his participation in a high-profile symposium at Casa Árabe in Madrid, Dr. Wesam Amer spoke with Palestine TV about Western media coverage of the Gaza war. Drawing on his research in political communication and media discourse, he examined narrative framing, information bias, and the broader implications of conflict reporting for international public opinion.

Dr. Wesam Amer participated in an interview with Palestine TV during his visit to Casa Árabe in Madrid, where he took part in a public symposium examining international media coverage of the Gaza war. The event brought together academics, media professionals, and members of the public to discuss how conflict narratives are constructed and disseminated across global media platforms.

During the interview, Dr. Amer reflected on themes explored in his presentation, which focused on the role of Western media in shaping public perceptions of the Gaza war. Drawing on ongoing research in communication and media studies, he discussed how language, framing choices, sourcing practices, and editorial priorities influence audiences’ understanding of events in conflict zones.

The symposium at Casa Árabe featured Dr. Amer's lecture, “Insights from International Media Coverage of the Israeli War on Gaza 2023–2025: Public Opinion Fabrication and Western Audience Engineering.” The presentation examined media coverage across major Western outlets and explored how narratives are produced, circulated, and contested within contemporary information environments.

In his Palestine TV interview, Dr. Amer highlighted the importance of critical media literacy and the need for rigorous, evidence-based journalism during times of war. He emphasized that communication is not merely a reflection of events but also a powerful force in shaping international awareness, political responses, and public debate. His comments underscored the responsibility of journalists, researchers, and academic institutions to engage critically with information and contribute to informed discussion.

The interview formed part of broader public engagement activities surrounding the Madrid symposium and contributed to ongoing international conversations about media ethics, conflict reporting, and the role of communication in contemporary warfare. Through both the lecture and the television interview, Dr. Amer brought academic research into a wider public arena, helping to bridge scholarly analysis and public discourse.
