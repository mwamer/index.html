Services

Academic & professional expertise

I offer research, analysis, training and advisory support to individual researchers, research teams and institutions. The four areas below describe the work I undertake, drawing on more than fifteen years of international research, teaching, academic leadership and policy engagement. Each service is delivered within my areas of verified academic expertise.

Research & Grants

Research design, methodology, research development, grant-proposal preparation and support for individual researchers and research teams.

Who it is for: early-career and established researchers, research teams, and institutions preparing or developing research projects and funding applications.

Areas of support: research design and methodology; empirical and qualitative research planning; grant- and fellowship-proposal development; framing research questions and impact; publication and dissemination planning.

Enquire about this service →

Media & Comms Analysis

Political communication, conflict communication, media analysis, strategic briefings and communication research.

Who it is for: media organisations, policy bodies, civil-society organisations, and academic and professional partners requiring expert analysis of media and political communication.

Areas of support: political and conflict-communication analysis; media and discourse analysis; briefings and expert commentary; research on digital platforms, AI and information environments.

Enquire about this service →

Higher-Ed Development

Curriculum design, academic leadership support, institutional development, higher education strategy and academic resilience.

Who it is for: universities, faculties and academic leadership teams, including institutions working in challenging or conflict-affected environments.

Areas of support: curriculum design and review; academic quality and programme development; academic leadership and governance support; institutional resilience and higher-education recovery planning.

Enquire about this service →

Mentoring & Training

Research-methods training, postgraduate research mentoring, academic writing and research supervision support.

Who it is for: postgraduate researchers, early-career academics and research teams seeking methods training, mentoring or supervision support.

Areas of support: research-methods training, including discourse analysis and digital methods; postgraduate research mentoring; academic writing and publication support; supervision and examination guidance.

Enquire about this service →
