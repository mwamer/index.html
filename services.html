Services & Offers

Where academic research meets the needs of organisations

Drawing on research, teaching and academic leadership, I help universities, governments, non-profits, media organisations and private-sector teams turn evidence and expertise into practical results: clearer strategies, stronger programmes, better-designed research and more skilled people.

My focus is the Middle East and its connections with Europe. Larger projects are delivered through Frontline World, the consultancy I founded.

Research and Academic Consultancy

For organisations that need rigorous, evidence-based analysis of political communication, media, digital technologies, artificial intelligence and conflict. Institutional Development and Strategic Advisory

Process

How I work

Every engagement is shaped to the client's aims, audience and context.

  1. 1

    Scope

    A short conversation to understand the aim, the audience and the constraints.

  2. 2

    Agree

    A clear scope, outputs and timetable before work begins.

  3. 3

    Deliver

    Work carried out in the format that fits, with evidence-led analysis and sensitivity to context.

  4. 4

    Follow up

    Support after delivery, so that advice and training turn into practice.

Frontline World

Working with Frontline World

Frontline World Ltd, which I founded and direct, is the consultancy through which wider institutional programmes, strategic research and advisory projects, risk and strategic foresight, strategic communication and professional training are delivered. It provides an organisational platform for broader programmes, connecting research, insight, foresight and action.

Individual engagements such as lectures, expert comment, advisory sessions, mentoring and commissioned analysis can be arranged with me directly. If you are unsure which route fits, get in touch and I will advise.
