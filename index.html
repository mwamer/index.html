About

Dr. Mohammedwesam Amer is an established scholar working on critical issues in contemporary media, communication, and digital culture. His research addresses the role of media platforms, algorithmic systems, and communication technologies in shaping political discourse, conflict narratives, and academic knowledge production.

With expertise in critical discourse analysis, platform studies, and media theory, Dr. Amer engages with academics, researchers, policymakers, and civil society organizations to advance understanding of communication's role in contemporary challenges.

Research Programme

Platform Communication and Digital Culture

Examining how social media platforms, algorithms, and digital infrastructures shape political discourse, social movements, and collective meaning-making in contemporary societies.

Conflict Narratives and Media

Analyzing how media representations, military communication, propaganda, and narrative warfare function in contemporary conflicts, with particular focus on discursive strategies and counter-narratives.

Critical Discourse Analysis

Using critical discourse analysis and multimodal methods to examine power relations, ideologies, and knowledge production embedded in communication practices.

Artificial Intelligence and Communication

Investigating the implications of AI systems, large language models, and automated communication technologies for research, education, and public discourse.

Higher Education and Academic Knowledge

Examining institutional pressures, scholarly communication practices, and broader changes affecting academic work, intellectual freedom, and knowledge production.
