Affiliated Lecturer, Cambridge Digital Humanities

Dr Wesam Amer

Scholar of Media, Communication and Political Discourse

Dr Wesam Amer is a media and communication scholar whose research examines how digital platforms, artificial intelligence and political discourse shape power, legitimacy and mobilisation, particularly in conflict and crisis environments. His work spans hypermedia warfare, AI-mediated communication, platform governance and the reconstruction of higher education in conflict zones.
