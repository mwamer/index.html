Scholar · Expert · Consultant

Who can speak, be seen and know under conflict

Dr Wesam Amer is an academic, researcher and consultant specialising in political communication, media and digital technologies, artificial intelligence, and higher education in conflict-affected contexts.

He is Affiliated Lecturer at Cambridge Digital Humanities and Founder and Director of Frontline World, with particular attention to Palestine and the wider Middle East.

Research

Three connected areas, one question

Who can speak, be seen and produce knowledge under conditions of conflict and technological change?

I

Political communication, media and conflict

How political actors, movements and media institutions construct narratives and shape public understanding.

II

AI, digital technologies and algorithmic power

How platforms and algorithmic systems transform communication and the visibility of information.

III

Higher education, scholasticide and reconstruction

The destruction of educational institutions in conflict, particularly in Palestine, and how academic communities can be restored.

Two frameworks link these areas: Techno-Discursive Critical Analysis (TDCA) and de-scholasticide. Current projects include HYPERWAR, on AI-mediated hypermedia warfare.
