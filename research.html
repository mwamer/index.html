Research

Who can speak, be seen and know under conflict

My research examines how conflict, technology and political power shape the production, circulation and loss of knowledge. It follows two connected agendas, each with its own framework: Techno-Discursive Critical Analysis (TDCA) and de-scholasticide.

De-scholasticide is the study of how education is restored after scholasticide, its systematic destruction.

Three pillars

Research pillars and questions

I

Political Communication, Media and Conflict

How political actors, movements and media institutions construct narratives and shape public understanding in conflict. One strand studies mainstream media coverage of war; the other studies social media and digital communication by movements such as Hamas.

Guiding questions
  • How do media representations shape understandings of conflict, legitimacy and power?
  • How do Islamist movements use digital communication for political mobilisation and strategic communication?
  • How are terrorism, extremism and security framed in political and media discourse?
Examples
  • PhD on Gaza war coverage
  • Hamas in Cyberspace (2023)
  • Critical Multimodal Discourse Analysis (2024)
II

Artificial Intelligence, Digital Technologies and Algorithmic Power

How AI, digital platforms and algorithmic systems transform communication, political influence and the visibility of information, and what manipulated and multimodal media mean for conflict and security.

Guiding questions
  • How does AI transform political communication and the production of media content?
  • How do platform infrastructures and algorithms shape the visibility and circulation of political information?
  • What role do AI-generated, manipulated and multimodal media play in contemporary conflict and information warfare?
Examples
  • HYPERWAR
  • TDCA framework
III

Higher Education, Scholasticide and Reconstruction

The destruction and disruption of higher education in conflict-affected societies, with particular attention to Palestine, and what reconstruction requires beyond rebuilt infrastructure.

Guiding questions
  • How does the destruction of educational institutions affect academic communities and knowledge production?
  • How can universities maintain academic continuity under war and occupation?
  • What does de-scholasticide require beyond the physical reconstruction of educational infrastructure?
Examples
  • Beyond Scholasticide
  • Rebuilding Higher Education in Post-War Gaza
  • Re-engineering Palestinian Universities
Frameworks

Conceptual contributions

My research engages established theoretical traditions while developing analytical approaches to emerging questions at the intersection of communication, technology, conflict and knowledge production.

Developing framework

Techno-Discursive Critical Analysis (TDCA)

Integrates Critical Discourse Studies, Critical Algorithm Studies and Critical Security Studies. It examines how narratives, platform architectures and algorithmic ranking together shape what becomes visible and legitimate in conflict. TDCA links Pillars I and II and is applied in HYPERWAR.

HYPERWAR: AI-Mediated Hypermedia Warfare
Developing conceptual approach

De-scholasticide

Where scholasticide names the destruction, de-scholasticide asks what recovery requires: academic continuity, governance, institutional resilience and the rebuilding of the human and organisational capacity of universities. It belongs to Pillar III and is applied to Gaza.

Selected research projects

01 In progress

HYPERWAR

AI-Mediated Hypermedia Warfare in Contemporary Conflict

Examines how political communication, AI-mediated content, platform infrastructures and algorithmic visibility interact in contemporary conflict, focusing on Hamas and Hezbollah across Telegram and TikTok. A cross-platform study applying TDCA.

Explore HYPERWAR project
02 Published work

Hamas Digital Communication

Examines the digital communication practices, political expression and discursive strategies of Hamas and associated media environments. Unlike HYPERWAR, it is a single-actor study of political discourse.

Evidence: Hamas in Cyberspace (2023) · Critical Multimodal Discourse Analysis (2024)
03 In progress

Beyond Scholasticide

De-scholasticide and the reconstruction of higher education in Gaza, examining academic continuity, institutional recovery and the restoration of knowledge production.

04 In progress

Rebuilding Palestinian Higher Education

Research on the institutional, academic and organisational requirements for rebuilding higher education after the destruction of universities. It covers institutional transformation, business process re-engineering and organisational resilience.
